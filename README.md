`Playwright` · `Generative AI` · `Claude Code`

- Request portal that turns the manual help-centre article process into a ticketed workflow
- Requesters submit a source document, language and title (or an AI-recommended one) and preview an AI-generated draft
- Reviewers get emailed, claim tickets, classify them with reason codes & categories, then approve and push to the CMS
- Playwright automation publishes approved articles into the CS portal; SeaTalk bot for ticket notifications

### Photomation — Photo Culling & Editing Engine

- Culls a shoot three ways: sharpness (variance of the Laplacian), near-duplicate bursts (64-bit difference hash), and exposure — **56 photos down to 16 decisions**
- Automatic editing with a photographer's limits: reads the exposure triangle from EXIF, protects highlights by rendering and measuring, lifts sky and ground separately
- 27 film and photographer presets, 3D `.cube` LUT grading, EXIF-preserving 4:4:4 export
- Swappable local/S3 storage, HTTP Basic auth, gunicorn container, GitHub Actions CI
